# Acme Corp - Remote Work & Employee Handbook 2026

## 1. Work Hours & Remote Guidelines
Acme Corp operates on a flexible core-hours policy. All full-time team members are expected to be available for sync calls between **10:00 AM and 3:00 PM EST**. Outside core hours, work schedules are asynchronous.

### Core Expectations
- **Communication**: Primary async communication occurs in Slack (`#general`, `#engineering`, `#product`).
- **Response Times**: Internal messages should be acknowledged within **4 hours** during business days.
- **Status Updates**: Daily check-ins posted in team Slack channels before 11:00 AM.

---

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Policy

### Vacation Allowance
- All full-time employees receive **25 days of paid vacation** per calendar year.
- Unused vacation up to **5 days** can be rolled over into the next calendar year.
- Vacation requests exceeding **3 consecutive days** must be submitted via BambooHR at least **2 weeks in advance**.

### Sick & Mental Health Days
- Employees are granted **10 paid sick days** annually.
- No medical doctor's note is required for absences under 3 consecutive business days.

---

## 3. Technology & Equipment Allowance

### Initial Setup Hardware
Upon joining, employees receive:
- **Laptop**: 16-inch Apple MacBook Pro M3 Max or Dell XPS 15 (Developer Edition).
- **Monitor**: 34-inch 4K Curved Display or two 27-inch 4K Monitors.
- **Peripherals**: Ergonomic Keyboard, Precision Mouse, and Noise-Cancelling Headset (Sony WH-1000XM5).

### Annual Stipends
- **Home Office Allowance**: **$1,000 USD** per year for desk, ergonomic chair, or high-speed internet upgrades.
- **Learning & Conference Stipend**: **$1,500 USD** per year for books, online courses (Udemy, Coursera), or technical conference tickets.

---

## 4. Security & Compliance Requirements

1. **Password Management**: All accounts must use 1Password with 16+ character auto-generated passwords.
2. **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: Mandatory hardware security keys (YubiKey) or TOTP apps (1Password/Google Authenticator). SMS 2FA is prohibited.
3. **Data Privacy**: No confidential customer data or proprietary source code may be uploaded to unapproved public AI tools. Only enterprise-approved LLM API endpoints (Anthropic, Voyage AI) configured through internal proxy gateways may be used.
